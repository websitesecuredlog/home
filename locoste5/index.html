Baked with Passion

At Lacoste Bakery, we believe in the art of traditional baking. Every morning, our skilled bakers arrive before dawn to craft fresh, delicious baked goods using time-honored techniques and the finest ingredients.

From our signature breads to delicate pastries, everything is made from scratch daily. We take pride in serving the San Antonio community with quality, freshness, and warmth in every bite.
