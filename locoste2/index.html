Artisanat Français

At Lacoste Patisserie, we bring the soul of Paris to San Antonio. Our master pâtissiers use traditional French techniques, passed down through generations, to create exquisite pastries that delight the senses.

Every croissant is laminated by hand. Every macaron is piped with precision. Every cake is a work of art.
